principles
01

boring is a feature

nothing changes between versions unless it needs to. a system you can forget about is a good system.

02

walls stay soundproof

no telemetry, no accounts, no "suggestions" in your menus. your machine talks to you and nobody else.

03

trim the fat

fewer packages, fewer moving parts, fewer things to break. light enough to feel fast on hardware that isn't new.
